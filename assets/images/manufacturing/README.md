# Manufacturing Photography

These photographs are authentic images captured at Savitri Timbers manufacturing facilities.

Purpose
- Build visitor trust
- Support the About Us page
- Support the Manufacturing section
- Support Product pages

Image Policy
- These are authentic photographs.
- AI may only improve presentation (lighting, colour balance, exposure, perspective, sharpness, noise reduction and removal of minor distractions).
- AI must never fabricate machinery, products, inventory, workers or manufacturing scenes.
- Preserve wood species, dimensions, manufacturing evidence and authenticity.
- Only use images that exist in this repository. Do not create placeholders or fictional manufacturing content.
