What Timber Can Savitri Timbers Supply?

Our Products page presents the timber species we can source and supply for project requirements. Availability depends on the species, specification and enquiry — not every listed timber is held as permanent stock.

Manufactured flooring is a separate capability. For Teak and Maple flooring produced by Savitri Timbers, see Flooring Solutions.

Flagship Species

Canadian Hard Maple

Canadian Hard Maple is Savitri Timbers' premium maple offering — valued for its fine texture, light colour and suitability for demanding hardwood applications, including sports flooring projects.

Maple appears in both our timber supply portfolio and our manufactured flooring range. Supply of maple timber for a project requirement is not the same as ordering finished maple flooring.

  • Premium / flagship maple category
  • Sourced through established Canadian supply relationships
  • Suitable for flooring and architectural timber requirements

Sourcing Capability

How We Approach Timber Supply

Savitri Timbers combines existing sourcing relationships with enquiry-led procurement. Our product architecture is built to grow as confirmed opportunities develop — including future international sourcing expansion — without presenting exploratory markets as established supplier claims.

Existing Channels

Species supplied through relationships already serving our customers.

Network Sourcing

Procurement through the company's broader timber sourcing network.

On-Demand Procurement

Material arranged against a defined customer specification and timeline.

Future Extensibility

Portfolio and origin coverage can expand when new sourcing is confirmed and approved for public communication.

Regional labels such as North America, Europe, Africa or other geographies describe sourcing context only. Specific country-of-origin claims are published only where business evidence and wording are approved.
