Flagship Flooring

Canadian Hard Maple Flooring

Canadian Hard Maple is Savitri Timbers' premium flooring offering. It is manufactured from maple boards processed through our hardwood flooring workflow for indoor sports and high-performance flooring requirements.

Flagship status reflects the company's maple flooring focus and established Canadian maple supply relationships — not a claim of exclusive availability or certified sports-performance ratings.

  • Flagship manufactured maple flooring
  • Finished widths: 65 mm or 92 mm
  • Finished thickness: 22 mm
  • Sold by square feet (SQFT)

Maple Flooring

Maple flooring is manufactured by Savitri Timbers as a core flooring product. Canadian Hard Maple is the flagship within this range; other maple categories may support flooring requirements according to project need.

Finished maple flooring uses the same approved finished dimensions and commercial unit as teak flooring. Raw maple boards enter manufacturing as 3″ / 4″ × 1″ material, 3 ft and above, measured in CFT.

From Board to Flooring

Manufacturing Transformation

Teak and Maple flooring follows a clear material path: incoming boards measured in CFT are processed into finished flooring sold by SQFT.

  1. Raw boards 3″ / 4″ × 1″, 3 ft+ · CFT
  2. Manufacturing Hardwood processing at Savitri Timbers
  3. Finished flooring 65 / 92 mm × 22 mm · SQFT
