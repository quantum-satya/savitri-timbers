Maple and Teak Entry Points

Canadian Hard Maple is Savitri Timbers' premium flooring offering, manufactured from maple boards processed through our hardwood flooring workflow for indoor sports and high-performance flooring requirements. Teak flooring is part of the core manufactured flooring line.
