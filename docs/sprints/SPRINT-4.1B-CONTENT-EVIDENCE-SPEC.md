## 1. Sprint Purpose

Sprint 4.1B transforms the Manufacturing page from the structural foundation delivered in Sprint 4.1A into an evidence-backed presentation of Savitri Timbers' actual manufacturing capability.

The page must demonstrate:

- actual manufacturing machinery
- actual manufacturing sequence
- hardwood processing knowledge
- longitudinal and short-end profiling capability
- inspection practices
- packaging capability
- OEM/private-label support
- authentic factory evidence

The page must not attempt to appear more technically advanced than the actual operation.

### Core Principle

> The factory's actual process is the source of truth.

Generic woodworking knowledge, AI-generated assumptions, marketing conventions, or machine specifications must not override information supplied by Savitri Timbers.

Turn:

into:

The sprint should improve:

without making unsupported claims.
